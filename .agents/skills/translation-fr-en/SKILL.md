# French → English

You are a **native English speaker** reading French as a foreign language. Load it with `translation-craft`, `language-en` and `language-fr`.

## Structural contrasts

- French `de` chains become noun stacks, possessives or prepositional phrases (`lien d'invitation à la session` → `session invitation link`).
- French `on`, `il faut`, `il y a` become `you`/`we`/`people`, `you need to`, `there is`; reflexive passives (`Ça se vend`) become English passives (`It's sold`).
- Long French sentences with subordinate chains: split into two or three English sentences; English prefers active verbs and shorter clauses.
- Gender and agreement disappear; recover meaning with a noun when needed (`prête` → `ready`, `Inscris-toi` → `Sign up`).

## False friends and traps

- `actuellement` = `currently`, not `actually`; `éventuellement` = `possibly`, not `eventually`; `librairie` = `bookshop`, not `library`; `sensible` = `sensitive`; `assister à` = `attend`; `attendre` = `wait for`; `location` = `rental`; `prétendre` = `claim`; `demander` = `ask`; `résumer` = `summarize`; `déception` = `disappointment`; `sympathique` = `nice`.

## Idioms and register shifts

- `Du coup` → `so`, `that's why`; `En fait` → `actually`; `Ça marche` → `Sounds good`, `Works for me`; `Pas de souci` → `No worries`; `Tant mieux` → `Great`, `Good`; `Voilà` → `There you go`.
- French rhetorical politeness (`veuillez`, `nous vous prions de`) becomes a plain `please`; keep the polite level, not the formula.

## Formats and typography

- Drop the space before `: ; ! ?`; `« … »` → `" … "` (US) or `' … '` (UK); `1 234,56 €` → `€1,234.56`; `16 octobre 2026` → `October 16, 2026` (US) / `16 October 2026` (UK); sentence case.

## Length and UI

- English is shorter (about 10–15 %): favour verbs for buttons and short UI labels (`Save`, `Delete`).

## Frequent decisions

- Choose US or UK spelling first. `tu`/`vous` both become `you`: carry the tone with word choice and contractions. `Enregistrer` → `Save`, `Supprimer` → `Delete`, `Paramètres` → `Settings`, `S'inscrire` → `Sign up`, `Se connecter` → `Sign in`.
