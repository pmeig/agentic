# Italian (it)

Reference for a native Italian speaker's judgement. Standard Italian (Italy); note Swiss Italian where it differs.

## Spelling and grammar essentials

- Gender (masculine/feminine) and number agree across article, noun, adjective and past participle with `essere` (`siamo andate`).
- Definite articles depend on the next sound: `il/i`, `lo/gli` (before `s+consonant`, `z`, `gn`, `ps`, `x`), `l'`, `la/le`. Prepositions fuse with them: `del`, `nello`, `sulla`, `dai`.
- Accents: `è` (is) vs `e` (and), `perché`, `più`, `né`, `là/lì`, apostrophe in elisions (`un'amica` but `un amico`). Accent on the last vowel for oxytones (`città`, `caffè`).
- Subject pronouns are dropped (`Vado`, not `Io vado`); object clitics `lo, la, li, le, gli, ci, ne` and their combinations (`glielo`) are essential.
- Subjunctive (`penso che sia`, `affinché`) is alive in careful writing; speech sometimes uses the indicative.
- `ci` and `ne` carry place/quantity (`ci vado`, `ne ho due`); double consonants matter (`pala/palla`).

## Typography

- Quotes `«…»` (also `“…”` online); space not before `: ; ! ?`. Numbers `1.234,56`, currency `25 €` or `€ 25`; time `14:30` or `14.30`.
- Dates `16 ottobre 2026`; months and days lowercase; UI labels capitalise the first word only. `e-mail` (feminine: `l'e-mail`), `email` accepted.
- `d` euphonic (`ed`, `ad`, `od`) only before the same vowel.

## Register and address

- `tu` (informal, friends, young apps), `Lei` (formal, singular), `voi` (plural). Pick one for the product; app copy for peers uses `tu` imperatives.
- Buttons: infinitive or noun (`Salva`, `Elimina`, `Accedi`); instructions to the user: `tu` imperative (`Inserisci un importo`) or impersonal infinitive (`Inserire un importo`).
- Italians value courtesy markers: `per favore`, `grazie`, `prego`; avoid blunt orders in errors.

## How people really talk

- `dai`, `boh`, `magari`, `figurati`, `allora`, `cioè`, `tipo`, `insomma`, `ecco`, `mica`, `ci sta`, `comunque`.
- Anglicisms are everyday in tech (`app`, `link`, `login`, `like`), alongside official terms (`accesso`, `collegamento`). Prefer the common one for the audience.
- Texting: `xké`, `cmq`, `ke`, `tvb`, `nn`.

## Regional notes

- Regional Italian differs in vocabulary (`pomeriggio/dopopranzo`, `anguria/cocomero`). Standard Italian is safe for products.

- Variants (locale code, e.g. `it-CH`): load `references/variants.md` when the project locale has a region or is unknown.
