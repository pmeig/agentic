# Promotion and communication

## 1. Brief (`promotion/brief.md`)

- **Audience**: segments, their problem, where they spend time, the words they use. **Goal**: one measurable objective (sign-ups,
  installs, demo requests, waitlist) with a deadline. **Budget** and **assets** available (site, store page, mailing list, community).
- **Positioning**: for whom, what, against which alternative, why us. **Promise** in one sentence; 3 proof points (a number, a
  demo, a testimonial) that are true today. **Tone** (clear, warm, expert, playful).
- Pitch in 3 lengths: 10 seconds (hook), 30 seconds (problem, solution, proof), 2 minutes (story with a demo).

## 2. Ideas (at least 5, ranked)

Mix quick wins (a day) and bigger bets; for each: idea, channel, format, effort, cost, expected effect, indicator. Proactively offer
ideas such as: launch / announcement post and thread, 30-second demo video, before / after, "build in public" series, short tips
videos drawn from real use cases, landing page and store listing refresh, waitlist and invitation mechanics, referral reward, beta
testers and testimonials, comparison page, open-source or template giveaways, newsletter feature, community AMA, directory and launch
platform submissions, partner cross-promotion, event or webinar, in-product announcement and release notes, press kit.

## 3. Channels and methods

| Channel | Format and rule of thumb |
|---|---|
| Own site / landing page | one promise above the fold, demo, proof, a single call to action, fast and accessible |
| App stores | name, subtitle and keywords (ASO), screenshots telling a story, preview video, release notes, reviews prompt at the right moment |
| Email | one goal per message, subject under 50 characters, clear button, segmentation, unsubscribe |
| Short video (Reels, Shorts, TikTok) | 9:16, hook in 3 s, captions, 15-45 s, one idea |
| Professional networks | story or lesson first, visual, no hard sell; threads and carousels |
| Communities and forums | give value first, follow each community's rules, disclose affiliation |
| Search (SEO) | one page per intent, useful content, structured data, speed |
| Launch platforms and directories | prepared assets, first comment, availability on launch day |
| Paid tests | small budget, 2-3 creatives, one audience at a time, stop rules |
| Partners, influencers, ambassadors | fit of audience, clear brief, disclosure of partnership |
| B2B outreach | short, personal, relevant, one ask |

Adapt copy, length and timing per channel; keep one message across channels. Local law applies: consent for email and tracking
(GDPR, ePrivacy), advertising disclosure, no misleading claims.

## 4. Calendar and measurement

- `promotion/plan.md`: date, channel, content, owner, link, status. Prepare, do not publish (the lead / user validates and posts).
- Tracking: UTM parameters on every link, one indicator per action, a baseline, and a review date. Report what worked, what did not,
  the next test.
