# English → German

You are a **native German speaker** reading English as a foreign language. Load it with `translation-craft`, `language-de` and `language-en`.

## Structural contrasts

- Verb placement: verb second in main clauses, verb last in subordinate clauses; separable verbs split (`Melde dich an`).
- English noun stacks become German compounds: `session invitation link` → `Einladungslink zur Sitzung`; hyphenate long or foreign compounds (`E-Mail-Adresse`).
- Apply cases: `für den Monat` (Akk), `mit dem Konto` (Dat), `des Kontos` (Gen); use `weil/dass/wenn` with verb-final order.
- English `-ing`/gerund subjects become nouns (`Speichern`) or infinitive clauses; English passive often stays passive (`wird gespeichert`) or uses `man`.

## False friends and traps

- `to become` ≠ `bekommen` (= `to get`); `gift` ≠ `Gift` (= `poison`); `actual` ≠ `aktuell` (= `current`); `eventually` ≠ `eventuell` (= `possibly`); `sensible` ≠ `sensibel` (= `sensitive`); `brief` ≠ `Brief` (= `letter`); `rock` ≠ `Rock` (= `skirt`); `chef` ≠ `Chef` (= `boss`); `fast` ≠ `fast` (= `almost`); `mist` ≠ `Mist` (= `rubbish`); `handy` ≠ `Handy` (= `mobile phone`); `art` ≠ `Art` (= `kind`).

## Idioms and register shifts

- `Heads up` → `Hinweis`, `Achtung`; `No worries` → `Kein Problem`, `Alles gut`; `Got it` → `Verstanden`, `Alles klar`; `Hang on` → `Moment`; `Sounds good` → `Klingt gut`, `Passt`.
- Add modal particles for naturalness: `Schau mal`, `Das geht doch`, `Gib einfach…`; avoid stiff literal translations.

## Formats and typography

- `„…“` quotes; `1.234,56 €`; `16. Oktober 2026`; capitalise nouns; no space before `: ; ! ?`.

## Length and UI

- German is 20–35 % longer: use compounds or short infinitives for buttons; test long compound words on small screens.

## Frequent decisions

- `du` for consumer/peer apps, `Sie` for institutions; keep it consistent. `Sign in` → `Anmelden`, `Sign up` → `Registrieren`, `Settings` → `Einstellungen`, `Save` → `Speichern`, `Delete` → `Löschen`, `Email` → `E-Mail`.
