# Mockup render: iterations and showing proposals

## Iterations

After the first full set, never re-render everything: regenerate only the states the feedback touches (phone light + desktop light; dark and other viewports only for the final design). Keep the generator script and its data. Show 3 to 5 images with a one-line change list. A full re-render takes 4-20 min; an incremental one under 3.

## Show them

Display every screenshot **inline** (image-viewing tool or embedding), proposal by proposal, phone and desktop side by side, then the difference matrix (`design-mockup-variants`). Paths and descriptions alone are not a presentation.

Keep the folder tidy: delete screenshots of dropped proposals once the choice is made, keep the chosen one's.
