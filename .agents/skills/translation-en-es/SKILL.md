# English → Spanish

You are a **native Spanish speaker** reading English as a foreign language. Load it with `translation-craft`, `language-es` and `language-en`.

## Structural contrasts

- English noun stacks become `de` phrases: `session invitation link` → `enlace de invitación a la sesión`.
- Add gender/number agreement; drop subject pronouns; use clitics (`Regístrate`, `Envíalo`); `-ing` subjects become infinitives (`Saving failed` → `No se pudo guardar`).
- English phrasal verbs become single verbs (`log in` → `iniciar sesión`); English passive often becomes `se` (`Se ha guardado`).
- Questions and exclamations need opening marks `¿ ¡`.

## False friends and traps

- `actually` ≠ `actualmente` (= `en realidad`); `eventually` ≠ `eventualmente` (= `finalmente`); `library` ≠ `librería` (= `biblioteca`); `embarrassed` ≠ `embarazada`; `exit` ≠ `éxito` (= `salida`); `to assist` ≠ `asistir` (= `ayudar`); `to realize` ≠ `realizar` (= `darse cuenta`); `support` ≠ `soportar`; `constipated` ≠ `constipado`; `carpet` ≠ `carpeta` (= `alfombra`); `success` ≠ `suceso`.

## Idioms and register shifts

- `Heads up` → `Ojo`, `Aviso`; `No worries` → `Tranquilo`, `No pasa nada`; `Got it` → `Entendido`, `Vale`; `Hang on` → `Espera un momento`; `Sounds good` → `Me parece bien`, `Perfecto`.

## Formats and typography

- `¿ ?` `¡ !`; `1.234,56 €` (Spain) vs `$1,234.56` (Mexico); `16 de octubre de 2026`; lowercase months; sentence case.

## Length and UI

- Spanish is 15–25 % longer: short verbs for buttons (`Guardar`, `Eliminar`), omit `por favor` on buttons.

## Frequent decisions

- Choose `es-ES`, `es-MX`, `es-AR` or neutral `es-419` first. `tú` for friendly apps, `usted` for institutions; Spain uses `vosotros`, Latin America `ustedes`. `Sign in` → `Iniciar sesión`, `Sign up` → `Registrarse`, `Settings` → `Ajustes` (ES) / `Configuración` (LatAm).
