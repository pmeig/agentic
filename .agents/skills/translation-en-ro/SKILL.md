# English → Romanian

You are a **native Romanian speaker** reading English as a foreign language. Load it with `translation-craft`, `language-ro` and `language-en`.

## Structural contrasts

- English noun stacks become genitive/prepositional phrases and use the enclitic article: `session invitation link` → `linkul de invitație la sesiune`.
- Add what English omits: gender/number agreement, the right case (`numele sesiunii`), clitics (`Înscrie-te`), and `să` + subjunctive instead of English infinitive (`Press to start` → `Apasă ca să începi`).
- Phrasal verbs become single verbs or reflexives (`sign in` → `conectează-te`, `set up` → `configurează`).
- English passive often becomes reflexive passive (`This is saved` → `Se salvează`).

## False friends and traps

- `actually` ≠ `actual` (= `curent`); `eventually` ≠ `eventual` (= `posibil`, `eventual`); `to realize` ≠ `a realiza` (= `a-și da seama`); `sensible` ≠ `sensibil` (= `sensitive`); `magazine` ≠ `magazin` (= `shop`; magazine = `revistă`); `to pretend` ≠ `a pretinde` (= `to claim`); `fabric` ≠ `fabrică` (= `factory`); `current` ≠ `curent` (also `electricity`).

## Idioms and register shifts

- `Heads up` → `Atenție`, `Ține minte`; `No worries` → `Fără griji`, `Nicio problemă`; `Got it` → `Am înțeles`, `Gata`; `Hang on` → `Stai puțin`; `Sounds good` → `Sună bine`, `Perfect`.

## Formats and typography

- Use `ș ț` with comma-below; `„…”` quotes; `1.234,56`; `16 octombrie 2026`; `25 de euro`, `20 de zile` (`de` after 19).

## Length and UI

- Romanian grows 20–35 %: prefer nouns or short imperatives for buttons; avoid long subjunctive clauses on small screens.

## Frequent decisions

- `tu` (`-ți`, `-te`) for peer apps, `dumneavoastră` for institutions. Keep widely used terms (`link`, `e-mail`) when users say them; use `aplicație`, `parolă`, `conectare` in formal text.
